# 3ball – Support

Need help with 3ball? Email **3ballballball@proton.me** and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

When reporting a bug, it helps to include your device model (e.g. iPhone 15) and what you were doing when it happened.

## Common questions

**I bought Remove Ads but I'm still seeing ads.**
In the game, go to **Settings → Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. Optional reward ads (like doubling your coins) are still available after buying Remove Ads, because you choose when to watch them.

**How do I change my ad and privacy choices?**
In the game, go to **Settings → Privacy Settings**.

**I saw an inappropriate ad.**
Go to **Settings → Report an Ad**, or email us with a description or screenshot.

**How do I delete my data?**
3ball doesn't store your data on our servers. Your progress is saved on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game. For anything else, email us and we'll help.
